CONCEPT 1 title "01 — Compact dropdown". Most conventional, restrained, recommended direction. White 64px site header logo left and close X right. Immediately below header, an inset white floating menu card with 16px side margins, 16px corner radius, thin border, very subtle raised shadow. Content-height card, never a full-height drawer.
